## Context

For my 5th year at Epitech, I joined Reliev as a work-study student, three days a week. I picked up the same assignment as during my [3rd-year internship](../stage-reliev/): improving the signal visualisation tool the R&D team uses to annotate epileptic seizures. The company and the tool are described in detail on that internship’s page.

## What I built

On top of continuing the internship’s work, I focused on two topics:

- **Comparing different types of signals**, including EEG signals. An electroencephalogram records the brain’s electrical activity; it is the reference test for studying epilepsy.
- **Optimising and refactoring the tool’s code.**
